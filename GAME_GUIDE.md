# 🎮 RootDown Valley - Complete Game Guide

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. **Run the game**: `python stardew_valley_game.py` or double-click `run_game.bat`
2. **Move** your character with WASD or arrow keys
3. **Select tools** with number keys 1-4
4. **Select seeds** with Q-Y keys
5. **Use tools** by pressing Space
6. **Advance time** by pressing N

## 🌾 Farming Guide

### Step-by-Step Farming Process

1. **Prepare Soil**
   - Select Hoe tool (press 1)
   - Move to empty tile
   - Press Space to till soil

2. **Plant Seeds**
   - Select Seeds tool (press 3)
   - Choose seed type (Q-Y keys)
   - Move to prepared soil
   - Press Space to plant

3. **Water Crops**
   - Select Watering Can (press 2)
   - Move to planted crop
   - Press Space to water
   - ⚠️ **Warning**: Uses aquifer water!

4. **Harvest Crops**
   - Wait for crops to mature (4-8 days)
   - Select Harvest tool (press 4)
   - Move to mature crop
   - Press Space to harvest

### Crop Management Tips

- **Check water levels** regularly
- **Water before crops wilt** (water level < 20)
- **Don't over-water** - conserve aquifer
- **Plan for weather** - drought doubles water loss
- **Diversify crops** - different water needs

## 💧 Water Conservation Strategy

### Understanding Water Mechanics
- **Aquifer Level**: Starts at 100%, decreases with watering
- **Water Usage**: Each watering uses 5 aquifer units
- **Weather Effects**: Rain provides free water, drought increases loss
- **Crop Tolerance**: Native plants need less water

### Conservation Tips
1. **Plant native crops** - they're drought-resistant
2. **Water efficiently** - only when necessary
3. **Monitor aquifer** - don't let it drop too low
4. **Plan for drought** - prepare for dry weather
5. **Use rain** - crops get free water during rain

### Native Plant Benefits
- **Native Flowers**: 70% less water needed, +15 score bonus
- **Drought Grass**: 80% less water needed, +15 score bonus
- **Higher value** when sold
- **Better survival** in drought conditions

## 🌤️ Weather System

### Weather Types
- **☀️ Sunny**: Normal water loss
- **🌧️ Rainy**: Crops get free water (no aquifer use)
- **🌵 Drought**: Double water loss for all crops

### Weather Strategy
- **Plan ahead** for drought conditions
- **Use rainy days** to let crops recover
- **Plant drought-resistant crops** before dry periods
- **Monitor weather** patterns

## ⚒️ Tools Guide

### 1. Hoe
- **Purpose**: Prepare soil for planting
- **Energy Cost**: 5 points
- **Use**: Till empty tiles
- **Tip**: Always prepare soil before planting

### 2. Watering Can
- **Purpose**: Water crops
- **Energy Cost**: 3 points
- **Aquifer Cost**: 5 units
- **Use**: Restore crop water levels
- **Tip**: Water strategically, not excessively

### 3. Seeds
- **Purpose**: Plant crops
- **Energy Cost**: 2 points
- **Use**: Plant selected seed type
- **Tip**: Choose seeds based on water availability

### 4. Harvest Tool
- **Purpose**: Collect mature crops
- **Energy Cost**: 1 point
- **Use**: Harvest ready crops
- **Tip**: Harvest immediately when ready

## 🎯 Scoring System

### Score Components
- **Base Score**: Days survived × 10 points
- **Health Bonus**: +5 points per healthy crop
