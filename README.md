# 🌱 RootDown: The Drought-Tolerant Lawn Simulator

A comprehensive environmental education game that teaches water conservation through lawn management. Build the most drought-tolerant lawn possible while avoiding aquifer depletion!

## 🎮 Game Overview

RootDown is an interactive simulation where players manage a virtual lawn with realistic environmental mechanics. The goal is to create a healthy, drought-resistant lawn while conserving water resources and avoiding environmental disasters like sinkholes.

## 🌟 Key Features

### 🌿 Plant Diversity
- **Traditional Grasses**: St. Augustine, Bermuda, Zoysia, Fescue
- **Drought-Tolerant Options**: Buffalo grass, Native grasses
- **Native Plants**: Wildflowers, Succulents (bonus points!)
- **Realistic Properties**: Each plant has unique water needs, drought tolerance, and root depth

### ⚙️ Lawn Management
- **Mowing Height Control**: 1-5 inches (taller grass = deeper roots = better drought resistance)
- **Watering Schedule**: 0-7 times per week
- **Fertilizer Types**:
  - None (baseline)
  - Organic (+20% water efficiency, +30% root growth)
  - Slow-Release (+10% water efficiency, +20% root growth)
  - Drought-Resistant (+40% water efficiency, +10% root growth)

### 🌱 Root Depth Mechanics
- Plants develop deeper root systems based on mowing practices
- Taller mowing encourages deeper roots (3-4 inches optimal)
- Deeper roots improve water retention and drought resistance
- Fertilizer affects root growth rates

### 💧 Water Conservation
- Realistic aquifer depletion mechanics
- Water usage tracking and scoring penalties
- Efficient watering based on fertilizer and root depth
- Sinkhole formation when aquifer is critically low (<20%)

### 🏆 Scoring System
- **Base Score**: Days survived × 10 points
- **Health Bonus**: +5 points per healthy plant
- **Native Plant Bonus**: +10 points per native plant
- **Root Depth Bonus**: +3 points per inch of average root depth
- **Water Penalty**: -2 points per water unit used
- **High Score Tracking**: Persistent across sessions

### 🌪️ Environmental Events
- **Sinkholes**: Appear when aquifer drops below 20%
- **Plant Health**: Affected by moisture levels and care practices
- **Drought Simulation**: Realistic water loss based on plant types and conditions

## 🚀 How to Play

1. **Start**: Click "New Game" to begin
2. **Plant Selection**: Click a lawn tile, then choose a plant type
3. **Water Management**: Use "Water Lawn" sparingly to maintain plant health
4. **Mowing**: Click "Mow Lawn" to reset mowing timer
5. **Settings**: Adjust mowing height, watering schedule, and fertilizer
6. **Survive**: Advance days and maintain lawn health
7. **Score**: Maximize points through water conservation and native plants

## 🎨 Game Mechanics

### Plant Health System
- **Moisture**: 0-100% (affects plant survival)
- **Health**: 0-100% (overall plant condition)
- **Root Depth**: 1-10 inches (improves water retention)

### Environmental Factors
- **Drought Tolerance**: Reduces water loss (0-9 scale)
- **Root Depth**: Improves water retention
- **Fertilizer**: Affects water efficiency and root growth
- **Mowing Height**: Influences root development

### Win/Lose Conditions
- **Game Over**: Aquifer depleted OR all plants dead
- **Success**: Survive as many days as possible with high score

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Install Python 3.7+
2. Install Flask: `pip install flask`
3. Run the game: `python app.py`
4. Open browser to `http://localhost:5000`
5. Start building your drought-tolerant lawn!
